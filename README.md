# Employee Attrition Prediction

## Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition data to identify workforce factors associated with employee turnover and develop predictive models that estimate attrition risk. The project demonstrates the complete analytics lifecycle, including data preparation, exploratory data analysis, predictive modeling, visualization, and business communication.

The analysis was completed as part of a Bachelor of Science in Data Analytics program and showcases practical applications of data analytics techniques used to support organizational decision-making.

---

## Business Problem

Employee turnover can create significant challenges for organizations through increased recruitment costs, training expenses, productivity loss, and workforce instability.

The objective of this project was to:

* Identify workforce characteristics associated with employee attrition
* Develop predictive models capable of estimating attrition risk
* Provide actionable insights that support employee retention strategies
* Demonstrate the use of analytics to solve business problems

---

## Project Objectives

The project focused on the following questions:

* Which employee characteristics are most strongly associated with attrition?
* Can predictive modeling accurately identify employees at higher risk of leaving?
* What recommendations can be made to improve employee retention?
